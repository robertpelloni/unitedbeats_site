# UnitedBeats Vision

UnitedBeats is a cosmic audio-visual celebration of Detroit techno culture and electronic music pioneers. It synthesizes dynamic generative canvas physics, deep-space visual cosmology, and ambient streaming beats into a minimalist, zero-dependency, single-file browser experience.

## Foundational Pillars
1. **Zero-Dependency Purity:** Pure HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript running smoothly on any client without bloat or build steps.
2. **Unified Operations:** Centralized single-page control hub giving instant access to physics, souls, sound, and ecosystem telemetry.
3. **Respect to the Pioneers:** Curated memorialization of the artists and souls shaping the electronic Detroit legacy.
4. **Resilient Automated Deployment:** Continuous CI/CD deployment to Hetzner production infrastructure triggered seamlessly on push.
